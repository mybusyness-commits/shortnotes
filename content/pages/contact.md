## Get in Touch with the shrtnotes Team

We value your feedback, suggestions, questions, and correction reports. Whether you have found a typo in our formulas or would like to request high-yield notes for a new topic, we are here to help.

---

### Direct Inquiries

* **General Inquiries & Feedback**: `contact@shrtnotes.pages.dev`
* **Editorial & Corrections**: `editorial@shrtnotes.pages.dev`
* **Advertising & Partnerships**: `partners@shrtnotes.pages.dev`

---

### Response Time

Our editorial team reviews all messages within **24 to 48 business hours**. When reporting a factual or typographical error in a note, please include the URL of the specific page and a brief citation to help us verify and update the material swiftly.
