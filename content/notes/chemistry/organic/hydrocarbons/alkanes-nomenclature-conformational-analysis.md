## 1. Conformations of Ethane and Butane

- **Staggered conformation** is more stable than **eclipsed conformation** due to minimized torsional strain.
- Potential energy difference between staggered and eclipsed ethane is approximately $12.5\text{ kJ/mol}$ ($2.9\text{ kcal/mol}$).

### Newman Projection Stability Order
$$\text{Anti} > \text{Gauche} > \text{Eclipsed} > \text{Fully Eclipsed}$$

## 2. Ring Strain & Cyclohexane Conformations

Cyclopropane ($60^\circ$) and cyclobutane ($90^\circ$) suffer heavy angle and torsional strain. Cyclohexane adopts a virtually strain-free **chair conformation** with ideal $109.5^\circ$ bond angles:

```chem Cyclohexane (Strain-free C6H12)
C1CCCCC1
```

```chem Cyclopentane (Envelope Form C5H10)
C1CCCC1
```

---

## 3. Branched Alkanes & Isomerism

Branching lowers the boiling point due to reduced surface contact area and weaker London dispersion forces:

```chem Neopentane (2,2-Dimethylpropane, b.p. 9.5°C)
CC(C)(C)C
```

```chem Isobutane (2-Methylpropane, b.p. -11.7°C)
CC(C)C
```

---

## 4. Free Radical Halogenation Reaction

Alkanes undergo photochemical substitution with halogens in the presence of UV light ($h\nu$) or heat:

$$\ce{CH4 + Cl2 ->[h\nu] CH3Cl + HCl}$$

$$\ce{CH3Cl + Cl2 ->[h\nu] CH2Cl2 + HCl}$$
