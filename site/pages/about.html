About shrtnotes

Last Updated: 2026-10-17

Welcome to shrtnotes

shrtnotes is a free educational platform dedicated to providing students, educators, and lifelong learners with concise, high-yield study summaries and conceptual breakdowns.

In modern academic environments, textbooks and lecture slides often span hundreds of pages. While comprehensive, this density makes rapid revision and exam preparation overwhelming. shrtnotes was created to solve this challenge by distilling fundamental principles in Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and Biology into structured, distraction-free notes.

Our Editorial Mission & Principles

  1. Clarity Over Volume: We focus exclusively on core formulas, reaction mechanisms, definitions, and high-yield examination concepts.
  2. Distraction-Free Environment: We believe studying requires mental focus. Our pages are designed without unnecessary animations, clutter, or intrusive advertising.
  3. Rigorous Accuracy: Every topic is outlined using established academic textbooks, peer-reviewed scientific sources, and standard curriculum guidelines.
  4. Accessible to All: All content on shrtnotes is 100% free to access without paywalls or forced registrations.

Who We Are

shrtnotes is maintained by an independent group of STEM graduates, educators, and science enthusiasts. We continuously update and expand our notes library to cover new subjects and topics.

Editorial & Review Process

Every note published on shrtnotes undergoes a three-step review:
- Concept Condensation: Identifying the key principles, derivations, and formulas that students need for conceptual understanding.
- Verification: Cross-referencing against standard university and higher secondary syllabi.
- Formatting for Retention: Structuring tables, math formulas (via KaTeX), and bullet points to maximize visual retention.

Contact & Feedback

We welcome feedback, corrections, and topic suggestions from students and teachers worldwide. If you notice any typographical error or would like to request notes on a specific topic, please reach out via our Contact Page.
